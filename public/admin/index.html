That didn't save — but your writing is safe

This almost always means your login timed out, not that anything broke. Nothing you wrote has been lost.

  1. Click Reload below.
  2. Log back in if it asks you to.
  3. Open the entry again — it will still be there as a draft.
  4. Try Publish again.

Important: please don't click Delete on the entry — that's the one action that actually removes your writing.

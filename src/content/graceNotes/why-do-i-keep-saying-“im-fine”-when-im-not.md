“I'm fine.”

It's probably one of the easiest things to say when you don't know what else to say.

Someone asks how you're doing, and you smile.

You go to work. You attend class. You take care of your family. You reply to messages. You laugh with your friends.

Life continues.

But when you're alone, everything feels heavier.

Maybe you're exhausted from pretending. Maybe you've been carrying grief, relationship problems, financial pressure, family expectations or emotions you don't know how to explain.

And because you don't want to be a burden, you say, “I'm fine.”

Sometimes, we don't even realise how long we've been doing it.

Being strong doesn't mean you have to hide everything.

You don't need to have the perfect words. You don't need to know exactly what is wrong. You can simply say, “I'm not okay, and I think I need someone to talk to.”

That can be a beginning.

Whether you're a child struggling to express yourself, a teenager trying to make sense of your emotions, a young adult overwhelmed by life, a parent carrying too much or someone navigating grief, relationships or a difficult season; your feelings deserve to be heard.

You don't have to keep saying you're fine just to make everyone else comfortable.

There is strength in asking for support.

At Rooted in Grace, we are creating a community where you can have honest conversations, find understanding and grow through what you're experiencing.

You don't have to carry everything alone.

Rooted in Grace; Gentle growth for all hearts.

There is a particular kind of disappointment that comes from not becoming the person you thought you would be by now.

Maybe you are 20, 28 or 30. You imagined having a stable career, being close to marriage, supporting your parents and becoming a blessing to others.

Instead, you are still figuring things out.

You scroll through social media and see engagements, promotions and milestones. You are happy for others, but somewhere inside, you wonder, *"When will it be my turn?"*

And sometimes, the pressure becomes too much.

You withdraw from people, cry when nobody is watching, entertain unhealthy relationships or constantly please others just to feel valued. Perhaps you have turned to substances or distractions to quiet thoughts you do not know how to handle.

But what if the problem is not that you are behind? What if you have been measuring your entire worth against a timeline that was never designed for you?

Your circumstances may need to change, your habits may need attention, and some relationships may need boundaries. But none of these things makes you undeserving of love, dignity or support.

You can be grateful for what you have and still acknowledge that life is difficult.

And while you are trying to figure out your own journey, remember that the person smiling beside you may also be fighting battles you know nothing about. We can make society a little kinder by learning to listen without judgement, checking on one another and making it easier to ask for help.

You do not have to wait until everything falls apart to seek support. Sometimes, the first step towards change is admitting that you are not okay.

**A question to reflect on:** What is one expectation you have placed on yourself that is making it difficult to appreciate your progress today?

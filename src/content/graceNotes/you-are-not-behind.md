Sometimes we look around and feel like everyone is moving except us. Someone is getting married. Someone is getting promoted. Someone is travelling. Someone is building the business we keep dreaming about.

And suddenly, our own lives feel late.

But God has never asked you to live someone else's timeline.

*"For everything there is a season, and a time for every matter under heaven."* — Ecclesiastes 3:1

Your season may look different, but different does not mean forgotten.

**You are not behind. You are still becoming.**

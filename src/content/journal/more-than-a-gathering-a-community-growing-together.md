There is something beautiful about finding people you can worship with, laugh with, learn from and simply enjoy life with. And sometimes, the best way to experience community is to step outside the usual gatherings and spend time together.

That is exactly what the Rooted community got to experience during our visit to Pastor Kaara.

The day began at church, where we joined in fellowship before heading to his home for a time of connection, laughter and shared experiences. From the moment we arrived, Pastor Kaara and his family welcomed us with so much warmth and generosity. Their hospitality made everyone feel at home, and it was a beautiful reminder that sometimes, opening your home and making room for others is one of the simplest yet most meaningful ways to show love.

But the day was far from just a visit!

We had Bible trivia, and naturally, things got interesting when we divided ourselves into two teams. Suddenly, everyone had an answer, a strategy or a reason why their team deserved to win. It was a fun way to test how much we knew about Scripture while reminding us that learning God's Word can happen in joyful and engaging ways.

Then came Christmas charades!

If Bible trivia tested our knowledge, charades tested our ability to communicate without saying a word. There was laughter, guessing, acting and those moments when you are absolutely convinced you know what someone is trying to communicate, only to discover that you were completely wrong. It was one of those activities that reminded us how much joy can be found in simply being present with one another.

Beyond the games and laughter, one of the most memorable parts of the visit was listening to Pastor Kaara share testimonies from his younger years. His stories were inspiring, filled with experiences and lessons that gave us something to reflect on long after the day was over. Sometimes, hearing how someone else has walked through different seasons of life gives you the encouragement to keep going in your own. Those testimonies were a reminder that growth takes time, that God remains faithful through different seasons, and that the journey is worth staying on.

It was also beautiful to witness a pastor and his family welcome a community into their home so warmly. It made the gathering feel less like an organised visit and more like spending time with people who genuinely value connection.

And perhaps that is what makes community so special.

It is having people to pray with, but also people to laugh with. People who encourage you in your walk with God, but also create room for friendship, fun and unforgettable memories. It is realising that following Christ does not mean life has to be dull or that every gathering must look the same.

The Rooted community is learning that faith can be shared around a Bible, over a game, through a testimony, at someone's home and in the everyday moments that bring people closer.

We left with more than pictures and memories. We left with lessons, encouragement and a renewed appreciation for the gift of doing life together.

A special thank you to Pastor Kaara and his family for welcoming us into their church and home, for their generosity and hospitality, and for making the day so memorable. We are especially grateful for the stories and wisdom shared, which will continue to encourage us.

Perhaps the beauty of community is this: you don't just find people to gather with; you find people to grow with.

And that is what we hope to keep building at Rooted in Grace.

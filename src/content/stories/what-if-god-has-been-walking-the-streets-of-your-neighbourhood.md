Jackie lived in a busy apartment block in a typical Kenyan neighbourhood. The kind where everyone knows the caretaker, water shortages are a regular topic of discussion, and someone is always asking, "Unajua maji imekuja?"

One Thursday evening, Jackie returned home exhausted. Her day had been long. She had spent hours in traffic, squeezed into a matatu, and dealt with the kind of challenges that make you question whether adulthood was really worth it.

All she wanted was to get home, eat something and sleep.

As she approached the staircase, she noticed her neighbour, an elderly woman, sitting outside her door looking worried.

"Jackie, uko na simu naweza tumia kidogo? Nimejaribu kupigia mwanangu lakini simu yangu imezima."

Jackie handed her the phone.

After the call, the woman explained that she had not eaten since morning because she was unwell and could not make it to the shops. Jackie went upstairs, got some food she had prepared and brought it down.

It wasn't much, but the woman's face lit up.

"May God bless you, my daughter."

Jackie smiled and went back to her house.

The following week, Jackie had her own difficult moment. An unexpected expense came up, and she was already struggling to stretch her salary until the end of the month. She had prayed about it, but nothing seemed to be changing.

That evening, someone knocked on her door.

It was the young woman who lived two doors away. They only exchanged greetings occasionally.

"Jackie, I hope you don't mind. I heard you mentioning that you're looking for another job. My office has an opening, and I thought you might be interested. Can I send you the details?"

Jackie was taken aback.

She had not told this woman much about her situation.

A few days later, another neighbour helped her when her gas ran out unexpectedly. Small things, perhaps, but they kept happening.

And one evening, while sitting by her window, Jackie began to think about something.

She had been praying for God to send help, yet she had barely noticed the people He had placed around her.

Could it be that God was answering some of her prayers through the very neighbourhood she lived in?

We often expect God to show up in ways that leave us speechless. We expect the unexpected phone call, the financial breakthrough, the big opportunity or the miracle that becomes a testimony.

And yes, God can do all those things.

But sometimes, His presence is found in the ordinary.

In the neighbour who checks on you. The person who shares what little they have. The stranger who gives you directions when you're lost. The friend who calls at just the right time. Even in the person who needs your help when you are having a difficult day yourself.

Perhaps God is not only visiting your neighbourhood to bless you. Perhaps He has also placed you there to be a blessing to someone else.

So, before you conclude that God is silent, look around.

Who has He placed around you? Whose life could He be asking you to touch? And could someone in your neighbourhood be experiencing God's love through you?

Maybe God is closer than you think.

Maybe God is in your neighbourhood.

"The Lord is near to all who call on him, to all who call on him in truth."  Psalm 145:18 (NIV)
